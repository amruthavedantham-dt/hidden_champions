## Business-model / identity flag (important)
This session's research found the MCA-registered entity has only ~6
employees (per ZaubaCorp filing, Oct 2024) — starkly inconsistent with the
company's own "30+ year" manufacturing self-description. The company's own
"About Us" page independently confirms this: it describes itself as the
exclusive Indian representative/agency for five international brands
(INGYU, Henkel Sonderhoff, HK Laser, Costa Levigatrici, ETone), not a
ground-up equipment OEM. This profile is written on that basis and flags
it prominently rather than silently accepting the "Industrial Machinery
Manufacturing" sector label.

Private Limited" (CIN U34300TN2006PTC059709, Tamil Nadu, auto-component
manufacturer, ~254 employees, ~₹80.6 Cr revenue) surfaces in searches
under a similar name — confirmed via Tracxn to be a different CIN,
different state, different business. Must not be conflated with the
target company (CIN U02922KA1999PTC025662, Bangalore).
