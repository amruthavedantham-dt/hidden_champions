# Raghu Vamsi Group — sources for verification

## Corporate structure — the primary issue with this profile
Unlike Metlok's identity ambiguity (which was suspected but unconfirmed),
research here **confirmed** "Raghu Vamsi Group" spans at least four
separately-incorporated entities with their own CINs:
- Comprotech Engineering Pvt Ltd — U29199TG1976PTC002062 (flagship
  entity, matches the HVT row's own website comprotechengineering.in; a
  JV between Adani Group and "Raghu Vamsi Management Team")
- Raghu Vamsi Aerospace Pvt Ltd — U35122TG2015PTC100718
- Raghu Vamsi Siri Aerospace Pvt Ltd — U35900TG2016PTC111810
- Raghu Vamsi Machine Tools Pvt Ltd — U28299TG1992PTC013990
- Other named related entities (not independently confirmed with CINs):
  Siri Sheetmetal, Skanda Aerospace, WMT, ITP, Raghuvamsi Advanced
  Systems, Raghu Vamsi Shares Trading

No consolidated group-level financial statement exists publicly. The
HVT sheet's own row treats "Raghu Vamsi Group" as a single company_name
(no CIN given in the sheet either) — this profile follows that same
scope but makes the multi-entity reality explicit throughout, rather
than picking one entity and silently presenting it as "the company."
