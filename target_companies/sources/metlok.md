## Identity ambiguity — flagged, not resolved (important)
Research surfaced two similarly-named "Metlok Private Limited" entities:
1. A Bengaluru-headquartered one (Bommasandra, KIADB Industrial Area),
   founded-brand-year 1991, CIN U24295KA2005PTC036555, directors Amitabh
   Sinha / Rina Amitabh Sinha / Nishchay Sinha (family-run), website
   metlok.in per Tofler.
2. A separately-described Nagpur/Kalmeshwar entity, described by the
   researcher as making glass and construction adhesives, website
   metlok.co.in.

**The HVT sheet's own row uses CIN U24295KA2005PTC036555 (entity #1) but
sources its `product_improvement` and `gap_currently_monetized` claims
— an "exclusive R&D facility in Nagpur (Kalmeshwar), 8000 sqft" and a
"W-27 MIDC facility in Kalmeshwar, Nagpur" — to metlok.in (also entity
#1's domain per research).** This could mean entity #1 genuinely has a
Nagpur manufacturing/R&D site with Bengaluru as the registered office
(a normal Indian corporate structure), or it could mean the HVT tool's
own scrape conflated two different companies. **Not resolved in this
research pass — flagged as the single highest-priority item to check
before publishing this profile**, since it affects whether "Metlok"
in this profile even refers to one consistent legal entity.
