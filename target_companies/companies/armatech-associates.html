Part 1

Real product capability and export reach, a debunked group-affiliation claim

No CIN exists for this entity, consistent with either a sole proprietorship or a smaller partnership structure — meaning no MCA-filed revenue, PAT, ROE, or ROCE figures could be found from any source. The only revenue signal is a broad, unaudited IndiaMART GST-slab estimate of ₹5-25 Cr. GST registration confirms the entity is based in Maharashtra (Bhosari Industrial Estate, Pune), registered since July 2017.

It manufactures precision 'Crystal' brand magnetic and non-magnetic products, including electroperm magnetic lifters with Quad system technology for safety and power savings — a real, specific product line with 30 years of R&D history in permanent electromagnets and over 1,500 magnetic and precision measurement products developed since 1980.

A real ownership-structure conflict exists between two sources: the HVT sheet tags this company as a sole proprietorship, while an independent source (crystalarmatech.com) describes it as a partnership firm with promoter/Managing Director Amol A. Lunawat. Both are reported here rather than resolved by assumption. Separately, this session directly checked and debunked a specific claim: the moat source citing 'jhalawadgroup.in' implied a possible affiliation with a larger corporate group — but a direct fetch of jhalawadgroup.com (an Indore-based tooling-solutions distributor founded 1989) found no mention of Armatech, magnetic products, or Pune anywhere on the site, and no group-company listing exists. This is most likely a citation/scraping artifact in the original source data, not a genuine group affiliation.

Its export footprint is real and independently confirmed: up to 20% of production is exported to New Zealand, China, Europe, Singapore, Dubai, and Malaysia. Its self-claimed certifications (IS 8710:1978, ISO 9001:2000, IATF 16949) could not be independently confirmed on either the IndiaMART/ExportersIndia listings or the crystalarmatech.com profile page — the HVT sheet's own `needs_verify: yes` flag remains unresolved.
