Part 1

A certified capability, financials that stopped updating

The only revenue figure found — ₹70.6 Cr for FY ending March 2022 — is dated and unverified against a primary source; no PAT, EBITDA margin or ROE figure was found for any year, and the company's own founding narrative is genuinely inconsistent: 1989 per its own 'about' page, 1998 for the legal entity's MCA registration, and a 2005 date embedded in the HVT sheet's own differentiation claim.

Its GRS (Global Recycled Standard) certification and named status as an approved supplier for BESTSELLER's CTF (a real, identifiable European fashion group's compliance program) is a genuine, checkable customer-trust signal — though the HVT sheet still sources it to the company's own LinkedIn page rather than an independent confirmation.

It runs at 4M+ meters/month production capacity with 5M+ meters of ready stock across four warehouses, serving 500+ certified customers with 10 product families in-house — real operational scale, whatever the exact revenue figure behind it.

A 2014-dated industry article (outside this project's usual 'recent moves' window) described 80-85% share of India's warp-knit weft-inserted suit-interlining niche and ₹15 Cr+ invested in German weaving/coating lines — a real historical data point, but too old to treat as current fact.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The field is modest-sized and steadily growing, tightly linked to India's own garment-export base.

    Global garment interlining market: $3.65B (2025) to $4.67B by 2030 (5.0-5.3%/yr); India's shirt-interlining sub-segment specifically is estimated at ~$0.90B, ~20% of the global category, growing ~6.8%/yr.

  • The field is led by one dominant global player, and India competes on price-performance against it.

    Freudenberg is the acknowledged global market leader with 150+ locations worldwide — Indian players like Goodwear compete on price-performance for mid-to-high-end garment exporters rather than head-to-head on scale.

  • The only named comparable Indian peer shows a much smaller operation than Goodwear's own claimed capacity.

    Bindal Cotex Pvt. Ltd. (Delhi, since 1991/1993) operates at 21 employees and 500,000+ meters/month capacity — roughly an eighth of Goodwear's self-claimed 4M+ meters/month.

A large-scale interlining maker by its own claimed capacity, with a real named fashion-brand relationship (BESTSELLER) and genuine sustainability certifications — but financial reporting that stopped in 2022 and a founding story that can't settle on one year.
