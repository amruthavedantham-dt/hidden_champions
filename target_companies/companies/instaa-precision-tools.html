Part 1

A young tooling specialist, largely unverifiable this pass

Financial figures are low-confidence and conflicting: one aggregator source cites roughly $1 million annual revenue (2025) with 10 employees, while a separate estimate places revenue in the ₹10-50 Cr range — a wide, unreconciled spread from non-primary sources. No PAT, EBITDA, margins, ROE, or ROCE were found.

It makes tooling for CNC punch press and press brake machines — CNC punching, punch press, embossing, and die tooling — a real, specific manufacturing niche. Incorporated as an LLP in May 2022, making it one of the younger companies profiled in this project.

The central finding of this profile is a verification failure, not a debunked claim: the company's own website (instaatool.com) returned an SSL certificate mismatch on direct access attempt — the certificate is registered to a shared hosting provider rather than the company's own domain, suggesting site misconfiguration, expired hosting, or an inactive/orphaned site. This blocked verification of the AS9100 certification status (self-claimed as 'in progress' per the HVT sheet) and the claimed GCC/EU/USA dealer-onboarding activity — neither could be confirmed or denied this session.

One LinkedIn post referencing team recognition (circa 2024) was found, but its content could not be retrieved in detail — no other recent expansion, award, or contract-win news was located.
