Part 1

A deep-tech bet on the next curve

The financial trend here is genuinely volatile, not a clean growth story: revenue moved ₹394 Cr (FY24) to conflicting FY25/26 figures (₹383-506 Cr depending on source), while PAT crashed from ₹30 Cr (FY24) to just ₹6 Cr (FY25) before recovering to ₹42 Cr (FY26). EBITDA margin followed the same dip-and-recover pattern, 17% to 9% to 18%.

Return on equity has stayed thin throughout — 6% (FY24), a 3-year average of 4% — and ROCE only recovered to 7% in FY26 from 1% the year prior. Q1 FY27 shows the recovery continuing: revenue up 42.95% YoY, PAT up 140.3% YoY.

What the weak numbers don't show: it says it is the largest and only commercial Indian manufacturer of Structure Directing Agents (SDA) for zeolites, the world's second-largest SDA producer, India's largest producer of Electrolyte Salts (ESS) for supercapacitor batteries, and the largest domestic producer of glymes — self-claimed and flagged `needs_verify` in the HVT sheet, but backed by a DSIR-recognized ~36,000 sq ft R&D facility in Vadodara.

Product mix in FY26: SDA 41% of revenue, PTC (phase transfer catalysts) 23%, ESS just 3% today — the electrolyte-salts line is small but is exactly what the new capex is aimed at growing.

The board approved a new Greenfield unit at Dahej-III — ₹200 Cr, 344 KL of additional reactor capacity, ~21-month build, targeting ₹300 Cr of revenue potential — alongside raising the company's borrowing limit from ₹300 Cr to ₹1,000 Cr, subject to shareholder approval.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The core PTC/SDA field is genuinely niche, and estimates vary widely.

    Global phase transfer catalyst market: $1.25B (2024) to $1.68B by 2030 (6.0%/yr) per Grand View Research; other estimates range up to $2.4B by 2035 (6.3%/yr).

  • The adjacent market the new capex targets is much larger and faster-growing.

    Global lithium-ion battery electrolyte market: $6.5B (2026) to $20.6B by 2036, 12.2%/yr — and India's own electrolyte market is projected to grow even faster, ~18.1%/yr (2026-2036), cited as the world's fastest-growing region at 14.0%/yr.

  • Euro-7 emissions standards are a direct tailwind for the company's largest current product line.

    Tighter emissions standards are expected to sharpen zeolite-based catalytic-converter demand, the end-market for the SDA line that's 41% of current revenue.

  • Named IPO-offer-document peers are considerably larger and more stable.

    Aarti Industries, Navin Fluorine, Alkyl Amines Chemicals, Vinati Organics and Fine Organics were named as comparables at IPO; detailed peer financials weren't available in this research pass, but Tatva Chintan's own market cap (~₹2,582 Cr) sits above the named peer-group median (~₹1,213 Cr) despite its thinner, more volatile margins.

A company whose P&L has been genuinely rocky, betting real capex on an electrolyte market growing roughly twice as fast as its core niche. The 3% of revenue that ESS represents today is the whole thesis for where the ₹200 Cr is going.

Market data: Grand View Research, OpenPR/Future Market Insights (electrolyte). Detailed peer financials for the named PTC-adjacent comparables were not found in this pass — flagged rather than estimated.

Part 3

Constraints, read as opportunities

One clear place worth watching, already funded.

Velocity

The growing electrolyte-salts line

ESS is just 3% of revenue today, in a market (batteries/electrolytes) growing roughly twice as fast as the company's core PTC/SDA business, with India specifically cited as the world's fastest-growing regional electrolyte market. The Dahej-III capex is a direct bet on this line scaling.

Product mix: aggregated company/analyst material. Market growth: openpr.com, futuremarketinsights.com.

Possibilities, not prescriptions. Which would work, and how much, only Tatva Chintan's own data and people can tell. Each is small, made to learn fast.

Velocity

Track ESS as its own reporting line through the Dahej-III ramp

As the new 344 KL capacity comes online, report ESS revenue and margin separately rather than blended into the total, so the electrolyte bet's actual returns are visible before the next capex decision.
