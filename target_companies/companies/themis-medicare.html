Part 1

An asset base that just showed its first sign of turning

The multi-year trend is a genuine, sustained decline: consolidated revenue fell from ₹395 Cr (FY22) to ₹342 Cr (FY26), PAT collapsed from ₹73 Cr to just ₹1 Cr over the same stretch, and ROCE eroded from 30% to 2.74% — four straight years pointed the wrong way. But Q4 FY26 alone posted PAT of ₹8.89 Cr (11.62% margin), aided partly by non-operating income, and the branded pharma business grew 19% YoY on improved field-force productivity — the first clear break in the downward trend.

Founded in 1969 as Themis Chemicals, promoted by two Hungarian pharma companies (Gedeon Richter, Medimpex) alongside Indian company Chemosyn — genuine multi-decade heritage, not a recent entrant.

It holds a real, independently-verified patent count — 9 registered patents in the medical/veterinary science category — against a company-claimed '50+ patents granted' figure in the HVT sheet's own product_improvement pillar (self-claimed, `needs_verify: yes`); the gap between the two is worth noting, not glossing over.

Its API facility (Vapi, Gujarat) is WHO-GMP certified, and its Hyderabad formulations facility holds EU-GMP, USFDA, PIC/S and MHRA approvals — a genuinely deep regulatory footprint across four manufacturing facilities, with a 240+ product formulation pipeline and 6 APIs in development.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The field is large and growing fast, well ahead of Themis's own recent trajectory.

    Global injectable drug delivery: $633.77B (2024) to $1,034.78B by 2030, 8.4%/yr — the formulations sub-segment alone growing 8.8%/yr.

  • Listed peers in the same segment show what recovery looks like, at scale.

    Gland Pharma posted 22.31% Q4 FY26 revenue growth and ~26% EBITDA margin (base business ~38%); Venus Remedies improved ROE from 5.8% to 8.1% and ROCE from 8.5% to 12.8% year-over-year — proof the segment rewards a turnaround, not just growth.

  • Sterile manufacturing's high barriers to entry cut both ways — hard to enter, but also hard to lose share once qualified.

    Multi-year USFDA/EU-GMP inspection and ANDA/dossier filing cycles constrain new entrants — Themis's existing regulatory footprint (USFDA, EU-GMP, PIC/S, MHRA) is a real asset that a struggling P&L doesn't erase.

Four years of real decline, a genuine regulatory moat still intact, and a Q4 that finally moved the other way. Peers in this exact segment show recovery is achievable here — the open question is whether Q4 was the start of one.

Constraints, read as opportunities
