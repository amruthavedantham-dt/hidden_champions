Part 1

A real 45-year shop, but no financial trail to check it against

As a sole proprietorship, Imperial Products has no CIN and is not required to file MCA financials — so no revenue, PAT, EBITDA, or ROE figure could be found from any source, and none should be assumed. This is a structural gap, not a red flag in itself, but it means every claim below rests on the company's own directory listings rather than any independently filed number.

It manufactures hi-power and precision-engineered coil-processing machines — coil slitting lines, cut-to-length lines, recoilers, decoilers, surface grinders, cold rolling mills, strip straighteners, and bending machines — designed using CAD software (SolidWorks 2018) and CAM software (SolidCAM 2018) on in-house Vertical Machining Centers and Turning Centers, a genuinely modern design toolchain for a small shop of this size.

It holds ISO 9001 certification (2008 or 2000 depending on source) and markets a CE Certificate of Compliance for its products — though the HVT sheet itself flags this pillar `needs_verify: yes`, since it rests entirely on the company's own directory listings with no independent registry check performed.

Operating from roughly 1,000 sq. yard premises in Chandigarh with 26-50 employees, it is led by CEO J Singh and Marketing Director R Singh/Ravi Inder Singh, and describes serving 'clients all over the globe' — though no specific export country or export-revenue percentage was found to substantiate that reach.

A genuinely old, technically-equipped small shop operating in a real but thinly-documented niche — the honest read here is that there simply isn't enough independently verifiable data (financial or competitive) to say much more than: it exists, it has real CAD/CAM capability, and its own web presence needs fixing before anything else about its reach can be trusted.
