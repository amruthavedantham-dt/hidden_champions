Part 1

A real NRB Bearings subsidiary with genuinely healthy, verified financials

As a listed company (BSE: 505827), SNL Bearings has real, independently-audited financials rather than aggregator estimates: FY26 revenue ₹56.62 Cr (+10.61% YoY), net profit ~₹10.85-11 Cr, operating margin ~25%, ROE 14.8%, and ROCE 20.1% — genuinely healthy returns, not thin or negative like several other companies profiled this batch. The company carries near-zero debt as of March 2026, and shows a 5-year sales CAGR of 9% and 5-year profit CAGR of 12%.

The website field discrepancy flagged before dispatch is resolved: SNL's real corporate site is snlbearings.in, not nrbbearings.co.in (which does not appear to be an official NRB Bearings domain at all — NRB's actual site is nrbbearings.com). The underlying reason for the confusion is genuine, though: SNL Bearings is a 73.45%-owned subsidiary of NRB Bearings Limited, acquired in June 2000 when DCM Shriram Group and associates sold their 45.78% stake, and the company was renamed from Shriram Needle Bearing Industries to SNL Bearings at that point.

It is described as the only needle roller bearing manufacturer in Eastern India, operating from a 3.5-acre Ranchi, Jharkhand plant built on technology originally acquired via the 1979 INA Germany technical collaboration. It holds IATF 16949:2016, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018 certification — all confirmed directly on the company's own site, exceeding what the HVT sheet itself flagged as `needs_verify`.

It designs and builds its own special-purpose machines for bearing manufacturing and low-cost automation — a real, independently-corroborated capability tracing to the original German technology transfer, not just a self-claim. Market commentary (not a primary company announcement) suggests a strategic pivot toward EV transmission/chassis applications and investment in automated inspection systems, though this should be treated as directionally indicative rather than confirmed company roadmap.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • A market where vendor size estimates disagree by ~60%, but real, scaled Indian peers exist for comparison.

    Global needle roller bearings market: $5.27B (2025) to $6.81B by 2029 (6.6%/yr) per one estimate, vs. $8.636B (2025) at 4.84%/yr per another. India needle roller bearing market: $428M (2024), roughly flat.

  • Parent NRB Bearings is a genuine market leader, holding ~70% of India's organized needle roller bearing market — a real structural advantage for SNL as its subsidiary.

    NRB Bearings Ltd, founded 1965, is described as India's first mover and dominant player in needle roller bearings; recent commentary cites an 18% revenue jump alongside an aerospace pivot.

  • MNC-backed competitors (Schaeffler, Timken, SKF) trade at much higher valuation multiples than NRB/SNL, implying the market views them as lower-cyclicality, higher-growth businesses.

    Schaeffler India trades at ~61x PE and Timken India ~50.8x PE, compared to NRB Bearings at a lower multiple — a real valuation signal that the market currently prices auto-component-linked bearing makers like NRB/SNL as more cyclical.

This is one of the strongest financial profiles in the batch by conventional measures: real, audited ROE and ROCE, a debt-free balance sheet, and a genuine structural backstop from a dominant parent company. The website-field error at the source was worth flagging, but the underlying corporate story it accidentally pointed to — a real NRB Bearings subsidiary relationship — turned out to be a substantive, useful fact rather than noise.
