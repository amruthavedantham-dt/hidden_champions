Part 1

A deep-tech bet on the next curve

This is honestly a down year on the P&L: revenue fell ~16% (₹547 Cr to ₹461 Cr), PAT fell ~72% (₹53 Cr to ₹15 Cr), and ROE dropped from 54.75% to 9.15% — not a company to lead with growth-story financials right now.

What the P&L doesn't show: it holds 100+ patented technologies (including its own MVR-based Low Temperature Evaporation 'LTE®' system), a DSIR-recognized R&D center, and ISO 9001/14001/45001 certification — plus 500+ sugar-factory installations across 40+ countries.

In January 2026 it was awarded a contract by LanzaTech to build a commercial-scale 24,000 MTA second-generation ethanol facility in Uttar Pradesh, using up to 300 tonnes/day of sugarcane bagasse — expected operational within roughly two years, and framed by the company as part of its own 'SED Smart Village' concept.

The core business is still active in parallel: it delivered a falling-film-evaporator and spray-continuous-pan-chamber modernization enabling Parag Agro Foods' Ravadewadi (Pune) plant to expand to 7,000 TCD crushing capacity, cutting steam consumption from ~32% to 21-22% on cane.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The core field is mid-sized and growing at a healthy pace.

    Sugar processing equipment: ~$5.2B (2024) to ~$8.7B by 2033 (~6.1%/yr); the crystallization-equipment sub-segment central to SED's product line grows faster still, ~7.5%/yr.

  • India's ethanol-blending programme has been a major structural tailwind — with a fresh policy complication.

    India hit its E20 target five years early (2025) and targets E27 by 2030, with ethanol procurement earning distilleries ~₹1.96 lakh crore cumulatively — but a December 2025 government directive curtailed sugarcane-juice-based ethanol specifically, over domestic sugar-availability concerns, creating near-term uncertainty for exactly the kind of sugar-linked distillery capex SED builds equipment for.

  • The closest listed peer shows the whole segment under margin pressure, not just SED.

    Praj Industries (₹3,228 Cr revenue, FY25) saw EBITDA margin contract sharply from ~10.06% to ~4.79% by FY26, with ROE falling to ~2% — a segment-wide bioenergy-EPC margin squeeze SED's own numbers echo.

  • The LanzaTech deal points at a much larger adjacent market.

    Global biofuels overall: $90-167B (2024) to $131-285B by 2030 depending on definition — the 2G ethanol/SAF direction SED is now building toward is a different, larger order of magnitude than sugar-mill equipment alone.

A patent-holding equipment maker having a genuinely rough year in its core business, at the exact moment the whole bioenergy-EPC segment is under margin pressure — while placing a real, contracted bet (LanzaTech, 2G ethanol) on a much bigger adjacent curve.

Part 3

Constraints, read as opportunities

One clear place worth watching, already contracted and in progress.

Velocity

Scaling the SED Smart Village concept beyond one plant

The HVT row's own gap research names this directly: the LanzaTech-backed 2G ethanol facility is one build within a broader 'SED Smart Village' concept aimed at shifting carbon demand toward higher-value green chemicals, polymers, aviation fuels and e-fuels — a much larger opportunity than the single contracted plant represents on its own.

HVT gap research (ir.lanzatech.com press release).

Possibilities, not prescriptions. Which would work, and how much, only Spray Engineering Devices' own data and people can tell. Each is small, made to learn fast.

Velocity

Track the LanzaTech plant's economics before committing to a second site

Run the Uttar Pradesh 2G ethanol facility through its first full operating cycle, and use its actual unit economics — not just the contract win — to decide whether a second 'SED Smart Village' site is warranted.
