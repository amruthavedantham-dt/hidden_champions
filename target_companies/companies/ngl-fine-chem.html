Part 1

A wealth-creation engine, recovering from a real dip

The recent trend is a genuine recovery story: PAT fell sharply from ₹41 Cr (FY24) to ₹21 Cr (FY25, down ~49% on margin compression from higher expenses), before rebounding to ₹42-48 Cr in FY26 as revenue grew to ₹490-500 Cr and OPM recovered from 7% to 12%. ROE held up through the dip at a healthy 12-14% range across the period.

It pivoted to an almost entirely veterinary-API focus in 1996 and has since built a genuinely independent reputation: Forbes Asia named it to its 'Best Under a Billion' list three separate times (2015, 2016, 2021) — external recognition, not a self-description.

It runs five manufacturing facilities in Maharashtra (~25,000 m² total) with 70+ dedicated R&D professionals among 700+ employees, and holds 3-Star Export House status from India's Ministry of Commerce; its S-18 site became US FDA-compliant in 2025.

It's funding a ₹160 Cr greenfield expansion at Tarapur via debt plus internal accruals, aimed at a 50% capacity increase for new pipeline products — pilot batch production began March 2025 after regulatory approval, and CRISIL upgraded its outlook to Stable in 2025, reaffirming ratings.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The field is growing steadily, with India's own slice outpacing the global rate.

    Global veterinary API market: $7.93B (2024) to $11.79B by 2029 (8.2%/yr). India veterinary API manufacturing: $547.7M (2024) to $1,133.3M by 2033 (8.4%/yr), ~5.9% of the global market.

  • Government animal-health programs are a direct structural tailwind.

    India's National Animal Disease Control Programme targets vaccinating 530M cattle against FMD (200M+ already done); ~80% of veterinary drugs sold in India must now meet updated GMP standards, tightening the field toward compliant manufacturers like NGL.

  • The one clean listed peer shows how volatile this exact segment can be — reinforcing that NGL's own FY25 dip wasn't unique.

    Sequent Scientific (now Viyash Scientific post-merger) saw net margin fall from 8.6% to 2.2% and ROE turn deeply negative (-19% to -5%) before its 2024 merger into a larger integrated animal-health player.

A three-time Forbes-recognized veterinary API specialist that had one rough year and is already recovering, backing that recovery with a real, funded capacity expansion. The segment itself is genuinely volatile — NGL's own peer shows how much worse a bad year can get.

Constraints, read as opportunities
