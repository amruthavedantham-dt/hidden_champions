Part 1

A capability the market is moving away from — and toward — at once

Revenue grew 34% to ₹127.1 Cr, with a 5.23% net margin, 5.34% ROE and zero net debt — modest profitability, but real growth on a debt-free balance sheet.

It calls itself the pioneer of 2 & 3-wheeler carburettors in India, manufacturing over 2.5 million units a year for OEM customers including Bajaj Auto, Hero Motors, TVS Motors and Kinetic — a self-claimed differentiation flagged `needs_verify: yes` in the HVT sheet.

Its DSIR-recognized R&D status is the strongest-credibility fact in this profile: it's `registry-confirmed`, sourced directly to a government PDF listing, not a company claim — one of only two companies in this entire batch of 15 with that credibility tier (the other being Garware).

It has already diversified beyond carburettors into throttle bodies, plus non-carburettor components (alternator/starter motor covers and brackets, shock-absorber canisters/brackets, master cylinder pistons, brake caliper pistons) — a real hedge against the industry-wide carburettor-to-fuel-injection/EV transition, though no specific dated 2024-2025 product launch was confirmed in this pass.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The core carburettor market is structurally shrinking as a share of the parent category, even where the parent category is growing fast.

    India's overall auto components market is projected to grow ~14.8%/yr (2025-2030) — far faster than the carburettor sub-segment's own 3.8-6.5%/yr range, meaning carburettors are a shrinking slice of a growing pie.

  • The throttle body line it's diversifying into is exactly where the growth is.

    Electronic throttle bodies specifically are growing at ~8.2%/yr, more than double the carburettor category's rate — directly reflecting the BS-VI/EFI transition.

  • The closest listed peer shows what happens to a company that doesn't diversify fast enough.

    UCAL Limited, a direct carburettor/fuel-systems peer with a Mikuni JV, has swung into consolidated net losses (₹13.15 Cr in Q4 FY26) with ROE turning negative (~-7.15%) — a cautionary comparable that makes Spaco's own modest-but-positive, debt-free numbers look comparatively disciplined.

  • India's two-wheeler market alone still consumes ~8 million carburettors a year — the largest single-country base globally, meaning the core business isn't disappearing overnight even as it shrinks in relative share.

A debt-free, government-registry-confirmed R&D shop growing 34% a year in a category the industry is structurally leaving behind — while its closest listed peer shows what happens if that transition isn't managed. Spaco's own diversification is already underway; the question is pace.

Constraints, read as opportunities

Possibilities, not prescriptions. Which would work, and how much, only Spaco's own data and people can tell. Each is small, made to learn fast.

Track throttle-body revenue as its own line

Report throttle-body and other non-carburettor component revenue separately from the core carburettor business for a few quarters, to see the diversification's actual pace against the segment's 8.2%/yr growth rate before committing further R&D budget.
