Part 1

A wealth-creation engine, turning the corner

Revenue grew ~33% to ₹116 Cr, PAT roughly tripled to ₹3 Cr, EBITDA margin flipped from -1.6% to a positive 3.3%, and ROE doubled from 5% to 10% — a genuine, broad-based improvement across every metric in one year, not just top-line growth.

It positions on a specialty range of life-saving medicines — antibiotics, anti-neoplastic and cardiovascular drugs — from a WHO-GMP-listed facility in Navsari, backed by over 500 registered trademarks and a DSIR-recognized R&D unit; the HVT sheet flags both `moat` and `product_improvement` as unresolved despite `Verified` review status.

It's a genuinely independent, checkable trade data point: the HVT sheet's own gap research places it as India's #15 exporter of Amikacin (an antibiotic) by value, with a 0.9% share of that specific export category — small, but real and rank-ordered, not just a self-description.

It opened a new international office in Dubai, UAE in 2026, explicitly framed as a hub for Middle East and global expansion — alongside existing export relationships in Myanmar, Kenya, Ethiopia, Nepal and Egypt.

Part 2

The market it sells into

  • The field is large, and India's specific pricing structure shapes it.

    There is no formal 'life-saving drugs' legal category in India — the operative mechanism is the National List of Essential Medicines (NLEM) combined with DPCO price ceilings, covering ~15% of the pharma market by value; India's overall pharma market is ~$55B (2025), heading to $120-130B by 2030.

  • MSME status is a structural advantage in exactly this category.

    MSME pharma units receive EMD exemptions and a 25% procurement reservation under India's Public Procurement Policy for government/institutional tenders — a real, policy-level edge for a company Chandra Bhagat Pharma's size.

  • Listed peers in the same category show a wide range of outcomes, from distress to strength.

    Albert David Ltd is currently loss-making (₹334 Cr revenue, -₹1.49 Cr PAT); JB Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals, much larger, was acquired by Torrent Pharmaceuticals in 2025-26 — Chandra Bhagat Pharma's own improving trajectory sits between these two poles.

A small BSE SME-listed life-saving-drugs maker that just turned every financial metric positive in the same year, with one specific, independently-tracked export ranking to point to. The Dubai office is a real bet that this improvement continues outward, not just domestically.
