## Differentiation — real, substantial, licensed technology (NOW PRIMARY-SOURCE CONFIRMED)
- Phenol and Acetone technology **licensed from Kellogg Brown & Root
  International Inc (KBR)**; Cumene technology **licensed from UOP LLC**
  (a Honeywell subsidiary) — **confirmed directly from ICRA's own November
  2016 rating report** (read in full via the Read tool), not just secondary
  press. EPCM (engineering/construction) contract was awarded to
  ThyssenKrupp Industrial Solutions (India) Private Limited.
- **Real production scale, corrected against the primary source**: base
  capacity at commissioning (November 2018, not 2015 — 2015 was project
  promotion/construction start) was 200,000 TPA phenol + 120,000 TPA
  acetone. IPA plant commissioned April 2020; IPA capacity **doubled to 60
  KTPA** (not 80,000 as earlier estimated) after a brownfield expansion in
  December 2021. Phenol capacity was **debottlenecked in Q3 FY2025,
  enabling production of up to 350 KTPA of phenol** (not 330,000 as
  earlier estimated) — this is the current, correct figure.
- **The capacity-leadership claim now has a clean, strong citation —
  replacing the earlier shaky "5x / 60,000 TPA" framing.** ICRA's own
  August 2025 rationale states directly: *"The domestic demand for phenol
  and acetone far exceeds domestic production at present. Moreover, with
  limited domestic competition, DPL has been able to garner a significant
  market share in the Indian market"* and *"ICRA notes that the company
  has a leading market position in most of its product segments across
  the domestic and global markets."* This is a rating-agency-sourced claim,
  not a self-claim — use this framing instead of the unconfirmed multiplier.
  The original 2016 report separately confirms the same structural point
  at project inception: *"with limited existing domestic capacities for
  these products, DPL aims to act as an import substitute."*
- **Deepak Group R&D Centre**: a real, dated PR Newswire release
  ("Deepak Group Unveils State-of-the-Art Next-Generation R&D Centre to
  Pioneer Innovations in Chemistry") confirms a genuine R&D investment,
  though at the Group level, not confirmed as DPL-specific.
  Source: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/deepak-group-unveils-state-of-the-art-next-generation-rd-centre-to-pioneer-innovations-in-chemistry-302575780.html)
- **Parent (DNL) global positioning, from ICRA's own report**: operating
  in the chemical industry for "nearly five decades"; among the top five
  global players in xylidines, cumidines, colour intermediates, and
  oximes; present in 30+ countries including the US, EU, Japan, ASEAN,
  South Korea, South America.

## Moat — a genuine, strong credit rating (full rationale now read directly)
- **CONFIRMED, full text read: [ICRA]AA (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+** on ₹729.02
  crore of instruments, reaffirmed 11 August 2025 (outlook revised to
  Stable from Positive on 28 March 2025). ICRA is a real, respected Indian
  credit rating agency (Moody's is its largest shareholder). Outlook
  revision reflects the sizeable capex plans across the Group and the
  execution risk that comes with them over the next couple of years — an
  honest, analyst-flagged risk, not a red flag about mismanagement.
  Source: full ICRA rationale PDF, read directly (id=136957)
- **A genuine, tellable credit-improvement story**: DPL's very first ICRA
  rating, in November 2016 (before the plant was even built), was just
  **[ICRA]BBB (Stable)** — a project-stage rating reflecting "high project
  implementation risks." It's risen to AA over the ~9 years since
  commissioning — a real, primary-sourced de-risking narrative.
  Source: full ICRA rationale PDF, read directly (id=31583, Nov 2016)
- **Real expansion capex — larger than earlier estimated.** The Deepak
  Group's capex isn't just the ₹3,500 crore figure found earlier — per
  ICRA's August 2025 report, it's **₹2,400 crore under Deepak Chem Tech**
  (modular capex, commissioning over FY2026) **plus ₹11,000-11,500 crore
  over FY2026-FY2028** for a 165 KTPA polycarbonate resin capacity plus
  300 KTPA phenol + 185 KTPA acetone + 100 KTPA IPA, plus a bisphenol-A
  (BPA) backward-integration project. A notable specific detail: the
  polycarbonate resin plant's technology and equipment were purchased from
  **Trinseo Plc for $52.5 million** and are being **relocated from Stade,
  Germany to India.**
- **Fuller consolidated group structure than earlier captured**: Deepak
  Nitrite Limited, Deepak Phenolics Limited (100%), Deepak Nitrite
  Corporation Inc. USA (100%), Deepak Chem Tech Limited (100%), Deepak PMC
  Limited (100%), Deepak Oman Industries LLC SEZ (51%), Deepak Advanced
  Materials Limited (100%), Narmada Thermal Power Private Limited (100%).
- **Real, current macro risk, not company-specific**: ICRA has since put
  Deepak Group's ratings "on watch with developing implications" due to a
  West Asia conflict beginning 28 Feb 2026, which disrupted natural gas
  supplies and spiked prices — a real external risk. A new Equinor gas
  supply contract (from May 2026) is expected to help address this. This
  is fair, honest market/macro context — fine to include in the profile,
  unlike the company-specific legal items below.

## Company-specific legal/compliance history — kept internal only
- **Historical**: a landmark 2004 Supreme Court case (Deepak Nitrite Ltd
  vs State of Gujarat) found the parent company liable for pollution at
  the Nandesari GIDC Industrial Estate; a 2018 complaint also alleged a
  tanker releasing hazardous liquid near the same site. This is about the
  PARENT (Deepak Nitrite), decades-old in the main instance, and not
  Deepak Phenolics specifically.
- **Recent, DPL-specific**: Deepak Phenolics received an adverse GST
  appellate order (6 March 2026) upholding an Input Tax Credit recovery
  demand of ₹2.15 crore plus an equal penalty — a disputed tax matter
  (~₹4.3 crore total) that the company plans to appeal to higher
  authorities. This is routine-scale for a ₹5,860 crore revenue company
  and far smaller in materiality than the Iskraemeco situation, but the
  same principle applies: this is internal due-diligence context, not
  profile content.
  Source: [ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/deepak-nitrite-subsidiary-deepak-phenolics-receives-gst-order-for-2-15-crore-penalty/36570056)

## Financial snapshot — large, real, growing, NOW AUDITED-FIGURE CONFIRMED
- **DPL standalone, audited (from ICRA's Aug 2025 report):**
  - FY2024: Operating income ₹5,003.5 Cr, PAT ₹473.9 Cr (OPBDIT/OI 13.8%, PAT/OI 9.5%)
  - FY2025: Operating income ₹5,805.1 Cr, PAT ₹591.2 Cr (OPBDIT/OI 14.5%, PAT/OI 10.2%)
  - Revenue +16.0% YoY, **PAT +24.8% YoY** — profit growing faster than
    revenue, a genuinely positive, audited signal.
  - Total debt/OPBDIT only 0.2x; interest coverage 45.9x — a very clean
    standalone balance sheet.
- **DNL consolidated, audited**: FY2025 operating income ₹8,289.8 Cr, PAT
  ₹697.4 Cr — note consolidated PAT actually **declined** from ₹810.9 Cr
  (FY24) to ₹697.4 Cr (FY25), even as DPL's own standalone PAT rose —
  meaning another part of the group had a weaker year, worth being
  precise about rather than implying group-wide profit growth.
  Source: full ICRA rationale PDF, read directly (id=136957) — this
  supersedes the earlier Tracxn-sourced ₹5,860cr estimate with the exact,
  audited ₹5,805.1cr figure.
- This is a large, established, profitable-scale industrial subsidiary —
  not comparable in financial profile to any other company profiled in
  this batch except Iskraemeco/Inox Solar.
