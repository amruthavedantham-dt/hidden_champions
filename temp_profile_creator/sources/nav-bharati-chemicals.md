## Financial snapshot — UPDATED with FY25 data (2026-07-18, analyst-confirmed)
**FY ending March 2025** (confirmed via Tofler + TheCompanyCheck + Tracxn, all
agreeing):
- **Revenue: ₹31.88 Cr**, down 5% YoY (revenue CAGR −5%, 1-year)
- EBITDA CAGR: −13% (1-year) — still declining, though less steeply than
  the −54.46% seen in the FY22 snapshot
- **"Profit CAGR: +621%" (1-year) — flagged, not taken at face value.**
  FY22's net profit was already negative (net margin −3.33%, see below).
  A percentage "growth" calculated off a negative base year is arithmetically
  distorted and can look dramatic while meaning very little (e.g. going from
  a large loss to a small loss, or a small loss to a small profit, can both

  **locked/undisclosed** on the free tier — the direction (loss narrowing?
  turned profitable?) is not actually known from what's available.
- Employees: 25-26 (consistent across sources)
- Compliance status: "Active Compliant" per ROC records; last AGM 30 Sep 2025
- Open charges ₹5 Cr, satisfied charges ₹6.65 Cr with HDFC Bank
