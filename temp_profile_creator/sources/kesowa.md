## Differentiation
- **Core business**: a drone-data SaaS/DaaS (Data-as-a-Service) platform,
  not a drone hardware manufacturer. Two main products: **Drone Call
  Center** (on-demand aerial-intelligence platform, claims "20x higher
  resolution" imagery) and **ARU SKYNET/ARU Server** (enterprise drone-data
  management software — upload, organize, analyze, collaborate on geospatial
  data; cloud or on-premises deployment). Named AI features: **Tree Count AI
  Model** (forest monitoring) and **Visual Pollution Index** (pollution
  assessment). Credibility: self-claimed (company site). needs_verify: yes
  for the "20x higher resolution" figure and the AI models' actual accuracy/
  validation — no independent technical benchmark found.
- **Important distinction worth keeping straight**: MCA classifies the
  company's activity as **"IT consulting and support services,"** not
  drone manufacturing or aviation — consistent with Kesowa being a data/
  software layer over drone-captured imagery rather than a drone maker or
  registered drone operator itself. This matters because **DGCA (drone
  regulatory) certification, which would be a strong registry-checkable
  credential for a drone hardware/operations company, likely isn't the
  right registry to check for Kesowa specifically** — no DGCA certification
  claim was found for Kesowa in this pass, and none should be expected
  given its actual business model. Whether Kesowa itself operates drones
  (vs. only processing imagery its clients/partners capture) is not
  resolved here.
- No patents, DSIR recognition, or ISO/sector certification found. One
  source loosely calls Kesowa "a pioneering drone data startup" — this is
  marketing language/press-echoed, not a specific, checkable superlative
  claim (no "India's first X" claim with a specific enough scope to verify
  against a registry or named competitor). `unresolved`, not `commodity`.
