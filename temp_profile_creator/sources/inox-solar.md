# Inox Solar — sources for verification

**⚠️ Read this first: this may not fit the "Hidden Champions" concept.**
Inox Solar's parent, Inox Clean Energy, is mid-IPO, backed by CalPERS and
Adar Poonawalla's family office, valued around ₹70,000 crore, and already
extensively covered in the business press (see below). The series' whole
premise is "India's quiet world-beaters" — companies operating below the
radar. This one is the opposite of hidden. Worth confirming with you
whether this is intentional (e.g. a real lead came in specifically for this
entity) before I build the full profile in the usual voice.

## Identity — resolved, but three related entities exist; don't confuse them
- **The right one: Inox Solar Limited**, CIN `U35105GJ2024PLC156301`,
  incorporated **13 November 2024** (very recent), registered at 301, ABS
  Tower, Old Padra Road, Vadodara, Gujarat. **Wholly-owned subsidiary of
  Inox Clean Energy Limited**, itself the solar-manufacturing holding
  company under INOXGFL Group (the Gujarat Fluorochemicals/Inox Wind
  conglomerate). This is the entity behind inoxclean.com/inox-solar and
  the real, physical Bavla/Ahmedabad manufacturing plant (see below).
  Source: [Tofler](https://www.tofler.in/inox-solar-limited/company/U35105GJ2024PLC156301)
- **NOT the same company: "Inox Solar Energy"** (Tracxn), Rajkot-based,
  founded 2017, listed as "unfunded." Search explicitly describes this as
  "a separate entity from the main Inox Solar Limited operations." Do not
  conflate the two.
- **Also NOT the same: "Inox Renewables Limited"**, CIN
  `U40100GJ2010PLC062869`, incorporated 2010 — this is a different, older
  group entity (appears wind-related, not solar). Noted only to avoid
  confusion; not otherwise used here.
- Directors (confirmed via FalconEBiz, 6 total): Santosh Uddhavrao
  Khairnar, Bhupesh Kumar Juneja, Shailendra Tandon, Anas Rahmanjunaid,
  Rupal Anand Vora, Devesh Sharma (Whole-Time Director).
- Capital (FalconEBiz, most current): authorized ₹56.25 crore, paid-up
  ₹50.11 crore — a serious, nearly-fully-paid capital base for a 20-month-
  old entity, consistent with a large group's deliberate new vehicle, not
  an organic startup. ROC: Ahmedabad. AGM: 30 Sept 2025; financials last
  filed 31 March 2025.
- **MCA's own activity classification: "Electric power generation using
  solar energy"** — correct and sensible, confirming the earlier Tofler
  fetch (which showed "motor vehicle manufacturing") was indeed bad/
  mismatched data, as suspected.

## Differentiation / capability — real, substantial, multi-source confirmed
- **Manufacturing scale claim**: "scaled up to 6 GW of module manufacturing
  (3GW in India and 3GW in USA)" within about a year of inception, per the
  company's own site — targeting a further 4.8 GW module + 2.4 GW cell
  capacity across Odisha, Gujarat, and Assam facilities.
  Credibility: self-claimed (company site). needs_verify: yes for the exact
  figures, though partially corroborated below.
- **Independent press confirms the physical facility is real**: analyst-
  confirmed via [SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2025/09/22/inox-solar-begins-production-at-its-new-3-gw-solar-module-manufacturing-plant/)
  and [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/renewables/inox-solar-commences-production-at-solar-module-manufacturing-facility-in-gujarat/articleshow/124039217.cms)
  — two independent, reputable outlets — plus Business Standard and
  Renewable Watch found earlier. All report Inox Solar starting production
  at its Bavla, Ahmedabad plant in September 2025, first phase 1.2 GW of a
  planned 3 GW facility. This is genuinely third-party confirmed, not just
  a company claim.
- **Technology claim**: TOPCon modules (glass-to-transparent backsheet
  configuration) at the Ahmedabad facility, per the ALMM listing below —
  TOPCon is a real, current-generation solar cell technology, plausible for
  a new large-scale plant.
- No patent/DSIR-specific claims found in this pass; the R&D story here (if
  any) would need a separate check.

## Financial snapshot — parent scale is enormous; keep it clearly separate from the subsidiary's own numbers
- **Group/parent scale — this is Inox CLEAN ENERGY LIMITED (the parent),
  NOT Inox Solar Limited directly** — important to keep this distinction
  clear in the profile, confirmed by the analyst directly against the
  [Economic Times report](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/renewables/adar-poonawalla-family-office-invests-rs-700-crore-in-inox-clean/articleshow/132132364.cms):
  - Inox Clean Energy raised ₹3,100 crore in equity (Jan 2026) from
    CalPERS, SUN Group Global, and Authum Investments, at ~₹50,000 crore
    pre-money valuation.
  - A further ₹700 crore into **Inox Clean Energy** from Adar Poonawalla
    Family Office, valuing that parent company at **₹70,000 crore**.
  - Inox Clean Energy filed a confidential DRHP with SEBI for a ₹6,000-
    10,000 crore IPO, expected to list in 2026.
  - Inox Clean Energy is committing ~₹1 lakh crore in total planned
    investment (renewables IPP + integrated solar manufacturing + energy
    storage) by FY28, targeting 10 GW IPP capacity + 11 GW solar
    manufacturing capacity, projecting ₹30,000 crore in consolidated
    annual revenue at scale.
  Sources: Univest, Groww, WindInsider, EQ Mag, Economic Times.
- **Inox Solar Limited's own standalone financials**: Tracxn shows
  **revenue under ₹10 crore for FY ending 31 March 2025.** This is
  coherent, not concerning — the Ahmedabad plant only began commercial
  production in September 2025, which falls in FY26, after this reported
  period closed. A pre-production entity reporting minimal FY25 revenue is
  exactly what you'd expect, not a red flag.
- The earlier Tofler-sourced "net profit margin of 7.03%" figure and
  "motor vehicle manufacturing" classification are **rejected** — the
  classification is confirmed wrong (FalconEBiz correctly shows "Electric
  power generation using solar energy"), and the margin figure matched
  patterns seen from the same AI-mediated fetch tool on unrelated
  companies earlier in this project. Do not use it.

## Alignment
- Capability: large-scale, ALMM-qualified domestic module manufacturing →
  Need: India's government-tendered solar capacity build-out, which
  requires ALMM-listed suppliers. Pull: must-have (a hard gate, not a nice-
  to-have, for government/PSU tenders specifically).
- Capability: US manufacturing footprint (3GW claimed) → Need: US market
  access / potential tariff-advantaged supply. Pull: not sized here.
- Group synergy (hybrid wind+solar via Inox Wind) → Need: hybrid renewable
  project demand. Pull: plausible, not independently sized.

## Execution gap
Genuinely hard to assess in the usual sense — this isn't an underperforming
or thinly-evidenced company; it's a well-capitalized, rapidly-scaling new
manufacturing entity inside a much larger, IPO-bound group. The more
relevant "gap" question here is execution risk on the announced scale-up
(6GW claimed to 11GW+ targeted) rather than an underused-capability story.
