# Iskraemeco (India) — sources for verification

**⚠️ Read this first: there is a real, current, market-moving accounting
controversy directly naming Iskraemeco — not just parent-company
prominence like Inox Solar, an actual disclosed discrepancy.**

A domestic brokerage (reported as Kotak Securities) flagged inconsistencies
in Kaynes Technology's FY2025 related-party disclosures **specifically
involving Iskraemeco**:
- Iskraemeco reported ₹180 crore in purchases from Kaynes Electronics
  Manufacturing that were **not reflected** in Kaynes Electronics
  Manufacturing's own disclosures.
- Iskraemeco disclosed year-end payables of ₹320 crore to Kaynes Technology
  and ₹180 crore to Kaynes Electronics Manufacturing, plus receivables of
  ₹190 crore from Kaynes Technology — **all missing from the parent
  companies' own disclosures.**
- The broker also flagged concerns about **goodwill recognition in the
  Iskraemeco acquisition specifically**, a rise in contingent liabilities to
  ₹520 crore, and a 17.7% average borrowing cost for FY2025.
- Kaynes' response: these were disclosure omissions in standalone
  financials (since rectified) — the balances were properly eliminated at
  the consolidated level under Ind AS but inadvertently not reported
  standalone.
- **Market impact was real and large**: Kaynes Technology shares fell
  ~11-12% over three days, wiping out roughly ₹10,000 crore in market cap.
Sources: [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/inside-an-accounting-storm-a-deep-dive-into-kotak-securities-allegations-and-kaynes-technology-responses/79073.html), [NiftyTrader](https://www.niftytrader.in/content/kaynes-tech-share-crash-kotak-jpmorgan-disclosure-issues/), [TradingView/Moneycontrol](https://www.tradingview.com/news/moneycontrol:022f27eb1094b:0-kaynes-tech-shares-tumble-12-sell-off-wipes-out-nearly-rs-10-000-crore-from-m-cap-in-three-days/)

**This is materially different from the Inox Solar situation** (which was
just "prominent and well-funded, not hidden"). This is a real, recent,
independently-reported, market-moving accounting dispute that names
Iskraemeco specifically. Worth your explicit direction on whether/how to
proceed — profiling this entity for outreach purposes while this is live

## Identity — two related but distinct entities, keep them separate
- **Iskraemeco India Private Limited** — CIN `U72909DL2019FTC356071`,
  incorporated 9 Oct 2019, ROC Delhi. This is the entity behind
  iskraemeco.com and the one acquired by Kaynes. MCA classification:
  "Other Computer Related Activities, N.E.C." Registered address: Eros
  Corporate Tower, Nehru Place, New Delhi (per one source) / East Delhi
  address (per Tofler) — two different addresses across sources, not
  resolved which is current.
- **Iskraemeco India Technology Development Centre Private Limited** — a
  SEPARATE, newer entity, CIN `U74909DL2024FTC439204`, incorporated 26 Nov
  2024 (i.e., right after the Kaynes acquisition) — likely a dedicated
  R&D/tech-development vehicle set up post-acquisition. Not otherwise used
  in this file; noted only to avoid conflating the two.
- **Directors — likely a stale pre-acquisition snapshot on Tofler**: Tofler
  lists Madan Mohan Chakraborty (MD), Bahaaeldin Abdallah Mohamed Baioumy,
  Maged Hassan Ahmed Abdelfatah — the latter two names read as
  Elsewedy-Electric-affiliated (Egyptian), consistent with the pre-Kaynes
  ownership. This page makes **no mention of the Kaynes acquisition at
  all**, suggesting Tofler's data here predates the Oct 2024 deal and
  hasn't been refreshed. Don't treat this director list as current.
- **Capital figures conflict significantly between two sources**: one MCA
  search summary shows authorized ₹1.2 Cr / paid-up ₹1.19 Cr; Tofler shows
  authorized ₹90 Cr / paid-up ₹43 Cr. Given the scale of the acquisition
  and likely capital injection by Kaynes, the Tofler figure is more
  plausible as current, but this is not confirmed either way — a real,
  unresolved discrepancy, not a minor rounding difference.
- Employees: 145 (as of 30 Sept 2024, per one MCA-derived source) — this
  predates the acquisition completing (Oct 16, 2024) by about 2 weeks.

## Differentiation — real, substantial, 80-year global heritage
- Iskraemeco (the global entity, Slovenia-headquartered) was **founded in
  1945** — 80 years of operating history. Owned by Elsewedy Electric Group
  since 2007, until the Oct 2024 sale of the India subsidiary to Kaynes.
- Manufactures smart meters, digital grid solutions, smart prepayment
  systems, smart water meters (e.g. "IskraSonic® 1000" — real-time
  diagnostics), and digital platforms (Symbiot software suite, eIoT EDGE
  compute module).
- Global reach: "6 continents," exports to "80+ countries" — self-claimed
  (global parent's own site), not independently itemized for the India
  entity specifically.
- **Certifications found — real, third-party, but ESG-focused, not
  technical/registry**: EcoVadis Platinum Medal (2026) — EcoVadis is a
  genuine, well-known international ESG/sustainability ratings provider;
  this is a real, checkable-in-principle credential, though not yet
  independently confirmed on EcoVadis's own site in this pass. Also GZS
  Chamber of Commerce Golden Innovation Awards, Family-Friendly Enterprise
  certification, Customers' Friend Certificate — all self-reported, lower-
  stakes recognitions.
- No patent-specific claims found in this pass.

## Moat
Global heritage (80 years), multi-continent export footprint, and
established smart-meter/water-meter technology are real, substantial
assets at the GLOBAL parent level. For the India entity specifically, the
moat question is really about Kaynes' own smart-meter manufacturing scale
ambitions — one search result quoted Kaynes management targeting "22 crore
meters" in India with "11 crore meters already awarded" (per a
Quora-hosted concall transcript — a real-sounding figure but sourced from
an unofficial transcript repost, not a primary filing; would need a real
Kaynes investor-relations source to confirm properly).

## Execution gap
