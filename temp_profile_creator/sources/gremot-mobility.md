## Differentiation
**Claim:** manufactures "Motors and Motor Controllers for Battery Driven
Applications," using Internal Permanent Magnet (IPM) motor technology
described as using "significantly less power than their competing
cousins." Vision stated (per Tracxn/YourStory-sourced summary): **"to be
the leading supplier for low voltage motors (<100 Volts) in India for the
light electric vehicle space."**
Credibility: self-claimed (company site + aggregator-sourced positioning
statement). needs_verify: yes — "leading supplier" is a superlative claim
needing the same scrutiny as any "largest/first/only" claim elsewhere in
this project. No patent or DSIR recognition found in search.
**Note:** unlike Konwert (a vehicle-level retrofit company) or Slidewell/
Dr. Sabharwal's, Gremot is a **component-level** manufacturer (motors/
controllers sold to other EV makers, not a finished vehicle or consumer
product) — no ARAI/AIS-type vehicle certification claim was found anywhere
on the site or in search, which may be expected for this tier of the supply
chain rather than a gap.

**"Leading supplier" — resolved via a real primary source, not an unsourced
answer.** A genuine 2021-era exhibitor-directory PDF (IIEV Show) pasted by
the analyst confirms the exact wording: *"Our Vision Is To Be The Leading
Supplier For Low Voltage Motors (<100 Volts) In India For The Light
Electric Vehicle Space... The Company Is Moving From Being In The Pure
R&D Stage To Thinking About Sales."* This is explicitly framed by the
company itself as an aspiration/vision, not a claim of current market
position — a well-behaved claim, unlike unlabeled superlatives seen
elsewhere in this project. No need to flag this one as an overreach; it's
honestly self-labeled.

**REAL, CONFIRMED innovation credential**: Gremot placed **2nd at iCreate's
EVangelise'21** (a genuine, dated EV-innovation challenge that began July
2021), for "energy recovery capabilities for EV motors" — winning ₹7.5 lakh
grant funding plus ₹50 lakh incubation support and iCreate facility/mentor
access. Confirmed via [ETN News](https://etn.news/iesa-contacts-menu/4497-icreate-announces-winners-of-ev-innovation-challenge-evangelise-21),
an independent trade-press report — this is a real, third-party-confirmed
R&D/product-improvement credential, the strongest such evidence found for
this company.

## Alignment
- Capability: low-voltage (<100V) IPM motors/controllers → Need: the light
  electric vehicle (LEV) segment (two/three-wheelers, light EVs) being
  electrified rapidly in India. Pull: plausible, strong — not yet sized
  against a market report.
- As a component supplier, alignment here is really about winning design-in
  relationships with EV OEMs, not direct consumer demand — worth noting
  this is a B2B alignment story, structurally different from the
  consumer/institutional-facing companies profiled earlier in this batch.

## Execution gap
Not much of a gap story yet — this looks like a straightforward small
manufacturer in real, if early, growth. The company's own described stage
("moving from being in the pure R&D stage to thinking about Sales," per one
summary) suggests the real story is commercialization ramp, not an
underused capability.
