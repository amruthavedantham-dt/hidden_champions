both retrofit kits — ARAI is a real, respected government-affiliated
vehicle-testing/homologation body, and this is a plausible, checkable claim
(unlike a vague "quality certified" line). **Not independently verified**
in this pass — ARAI doesn't have a public searchable certificate database
the way IAF CertSearch does for ISO; would need to ask the company for the
certificate/type-approval number directly, or check ARAI's own notifications.

MoRTH guidelines" — a real regulatory framework reference (MoRTH = Ministry
of Road Transport and Highways), plausible but not independently checked.
