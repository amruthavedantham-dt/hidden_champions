# Namhah — sources for verification

## Identity
- **No CIN exists, and that's correct, not a gap.** "Namhah Marketing" is
  registered as a **proprietorship** (not a Pvt Ltd/LLP), GST-registered
  1 July 2017, GST number `18AOTPB5899P1ZP` (Assam). The embedded PAN's
  4th character is "P" (Individual/Person), consistent with a
  proprietorship structure. Source: search-confirmed GST registration
  record + [ExportersIndia](https://www.exportersindia.com/namhah-marketing/), [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/namhah-marketing/) listings.
- **Founders**: Ankit Baid (CEO) — whose family has reportedly been in the
  Assam tea business for over six decades (a real, specific claim, plausible
  given the founding story, not independently itemized/dated) — and
  Abhishek Chowdhury (co-founder, reportedly spotted the price gap between
  premium Indian tea sold in Europe vs. domestic sourcing cost). Given the
  proprietorship structure is typically single-owner, it's not clear from
  public sources whether Abhishek holds a formal ownership stake or is an
  operational co-founder without a filed legal interest — not resolved.
- Founded 2015 (Tracxn, Inc42) vs. "Est. since 2016" (company site) — minor
  discrepancy, same founding-year-ambiguity pattern seen elsewhere in this
  project.
- Location: Guwahati, Assam — flagship retail store on GS Road, Christian
  Basti. Employees: ~5 (per Inc42) — a very small team.

## Differentiation
- **Claim**: direct sourcing from 16 tea estates/gardens across Assam,
  Darjeeling, and Arunachal Pradesh, bypassing middlemen; hand-picked by
  "expert pluckers with 15+ years of garden experience"; over 50 tea
  varieties. Supplies B2B channels (cafes, hotels, retailers, gifting
  curators, private-label brands) in addition to direct-to-consumer sales
  via Amazon, Flipkart, Snapdeal, and its own site.
  Credibility: self-claimed (company site). needs_verify: yes.

  anywhere — this is `unresolved`, not `commodity` (a specialty tea
  business could plausibly hold an organic certification given one product
  listing mentions "Organic Green Tea," but nothing formal was confirmed).
