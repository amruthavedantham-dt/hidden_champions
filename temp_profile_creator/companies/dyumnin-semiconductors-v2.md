## Part 1 — Real, checkable technical credibility, ahead of a financial trail

Dyumnin Semiconductors (OPC) Private Limited was incorporated in September 2018 in Bangalore as a One Person Company — a simplified Indian corporate structure for solo founders, which explains its very small ₹1 lakh capital base. The company was founded in 2016 by Vijayvithal Jahagirdar.

Founder Vijayvithal Jahagirdar built his semiconductor career at **Intel, LSI, and Texas Instruments**, where he worked as a Design Lead focused on processor memory interface verification, contributing to dozens of multi-million-gate ASICs across process nodes from **180nm to 6nm**, and authoring several papers and publications. He gave a public cocotb verification tutorial in October 2022 and maintains an active personal GitHub. Director **Soumya Bharathi** leads strategic planning and future products at Dyumnin, and brings extensive networking experience within the medical community.

Dyumnin provides full-lifecycle chip design services for early-stage startups — spanning chip architecture, algorithm modeling, verification, RTL coding, synthesis, timing closure, pre- and post-silicon validation, and firmware development. Its product and IP portfolio includes **Dikpalaka**, a perimeter security system for industrial and defense applications; a RISC-V server chip for data centers; ARM-based SoCs for embedded systems; **HFT-NIC**, an FPGA-based high-frequency-trading platform; EDA tools and reference designs; and RISC-V ISA extensions with AI-specific instructions and accelerators for edge AI and TinyML applications. The company also serves as a silicon advisor to **Simula.ai**, working on graph AI and hyperdimensional-computing methods.

The company's open-source work is public and active: **github.com/dyumnin** and the **Dyumnin-Interns** organization host real repositories, including **cocotb-ralgen**, a SystemRDL-to-raltest converter for cocotb — a widely used open-source hardware-verification framework in the semiconductor industry.

Dyumnin reports annual revenue of over **₹1 crore**, drawing on a client base spanning Asia (especially India), the US, and Europe.

While Dyumnin describes itself as "pioneering" in ASIC & FPGA design, its most distinctive strength is its early, public embrace of Python/cocotb-based open-source verification — a genuine, checkable specialization few boutique consultancies can claim. No patents or ISO-type certifications are publicly listed.

## Part 2 — A fast-growing, policy-backed market — and a very crowded one

- **The global semiconductor design services market is large and growing fast.**
  Valued at $8.2B in 2025, projected to reach $18.6B by 2034 — a ~10.3% CAGR. Asia-Pacific already holds 42.3% of global revenue.

- **India is already a major global hub for exactly this work.**
  Over 20% of the world's semiconductor design engineers are based in India, supporting R&D centers for Intel, Qualcomm, MediaTek, Nvidia, and Texas Instruments — the same company Dyumnin's founder came from. The India Semiconductor Mission has accelerated policy support since 2024.

- **But it's also extremely crowded.**
  India has 431 active semiconductor design and manufacturing services companies as of May 2026 — ranging from IT giants (Tata Elxsi, HCL Technologies, Wipro, L&T Technology Services) to specialized mid-size firms (eInfochips, Sasken, Tessolve). A one-person boutique competes in the same market as some of India's largest listed companies.

> *A fast-growing, policy-backed market where India is already a global hub — but one crowded with everyone from IT giants to specialized mid-size firms. A one-person boutique's edge here isn't scale; it's the kind of checkable technical reputation that's rare precisely because most competitors don't publish their work in the open.*

*Market data: Mordor Intelligence, IMARC Group, MarknTel Advisors, MarketIntelo. Peer landscape: Tracxn, Built In.*
